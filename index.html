The path

Sixteen lessons covering the official AZ-900 skills measured as of July 20, 2026.

  1. ☁️01What cloud computing actually isCloud computing is renting compute, storage, and services over the internet — plus how you pay for it, and what serverless really means.16 min
  2. 🤝02The shared responsibility modelSecurity in Azure is a split: Microsoft always owns the physical datacenter, you always own your data and access, and everything in between slides depending on IaaS, PaaS, or SaaS.18 min
  3. 🌐03Public, private, and hybrid cloudPublic is Microsoft's shared Azure. Private is a cloud for one organization. Hybrid is both, connected on purpose. Multi-cloud exists, but AZ-900 grades you on the first three.14 min
  4. 📈04Benefits of using cloud servicesHigh availability, scale-up vs scale-out, elasticity, reliability, cost and performance predictability, security, governance, and manageability — the reasons companies actually move.16 min
  5. 🍽️05IaaS, PaaS, and SaaSInfrastructure, platform, and software as a service — who manages what, when to pick each, and the classic VM vs App Service vs Microsoft 365 comparison.18 min
  6. 🌍06Regions, availability zones, and datacentersWhere Azure actually lives: regions, region pairs, sovereign clouds, datacenters, and availability zones — and how to pick a place for your stuff.16 min
  7. 🗂️07The Azure resource hierarchyManagement groups, subscriptions, resource groups, and resources — who bills, who organizes, and where policy and RBAC hang.15 min
  8. 💻08Azure compute: VMs, containers, and functionsHow Azure runs your code — virtual machines, containers, and Functions — plus VM options, the parts of a VM, and where web apps should live.18 min
  9. 🌐09Azure networking fundamentalsVNets, subnets, peering, Azure DNS, VPN Gateway versus ExpressRoute, and public versus private endpoints.17 min
  10. 📦10Azure Storage services, tiers, and moving dataBlob, File, Queue, Table, and Disk; Hot / Cool / Cold / Archive; LRS through RA-GZRS; storage accounts; AzCopy, Storage Explorer, File Sync; Azure Migrate and Data Box.19 min
  11. 🪪11Azure identity: Microsoft Entra IDMicrosoft Entra ID, Entra Domain Services, SSO, MFA, passwordless, external identities, and Conditional Access — who you are before anyone asks what you can do.18 min
  12. 🛡️12Azure access and securityRBAC, Zero Trust, defense-in-depth, encryption with Azure Key Vault, and Microsoft Defender for Cloud — plus a light contrast with Policy and locks so you do not mix the three tools.16 min
  13. 💰13Azure cost managementWhat makes an Azure bill go up, how the pricing calculator estimates before you deploy, what Cost Management does after you spend, and why tags are labels — not locks.16 min
  14. ⚖️14Governance and compliance in AzureMicrosoft Purview for data governance, Azure Policy for the rules of the estate, resource locks for the padlock, the Service Trust Portal for audit evidence — and the famous mix-up with RBAC, budgets, and retired Blueprints.17 min
  15. 🧰15Managing and deploying Azure resourcesPortal, Cloud Shell, CLI, PowerShell, Arc, ARM, ARM templates, and Bicep — and how they all fit together.18 min
  16. 📡16Monitoring Azure: Advisor, Service Health, and MonitorAzure Advisor for recommendations, Azure Service Health for “is my stuff on fire,” and Azure Monitor — Log Analytics, alerts, and Application Insights — for telemetry.18 min
